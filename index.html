ABOUT

Funtro International Company.

FUNTRO INTERNATIONAL COMPANY,

Funtro International Company is a leading Nigerian consulting firm dedicated to empowering organizations and individuals to achieve sustainable growth and operational excellence. Since our establishment in 2005, we have provided expert guidance in financial management, human capital development, legal advisory, real estate, and agricultural value chains. Our multidisciplinary team of seasoned professionals brings together expertise in management, accountancy, law, engineering, and more, ensuring holistic solutions for our clients.

We pride ourselves on our integrity, innovation, and client-focused approach. Whether supporting public sector reforms, driving private enterprise success, or delivering specialized training, Funtro International Company is committed to delivering measurable results and building lasting partnerships. Our mission is to be the trusted partner for organizations seeking to navigate complex challenges and unlock new opportunities in Nigeria and beyond.
